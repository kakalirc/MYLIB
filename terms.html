2. Who we are and what the app does

My LIB is run by LIBXL-Education Private Limited, No 6, 4th Floor, 2nd Main Road, Arakere, off Bannerghatta Road, Bangalore 560076, India ("LIB Education", "we", "us").

The app is a private tool for our tutoring service. It is used to:

  • Keep your username and password private. Do not share your login or let anyone else use it.
  • You are responsible for everything done under your login. Tell us at once if you think someone else has used it.
  • Give us correct details (name, phone number, grade, subjects) and tell us when they change.
  • We may change your role or access (for example, turning Question Bank or AI access on or off), or suspend or close a login, at any time, especially when tuition ends or these Terms are broken.
  • The app records each login and logout (time, user and role) for security.

4. Fees, payments and refunds

The app does not take money. It shows what is owed and records payments made outside the app.

  • How you pay. A fee request shows the amount and our UPI ID or QR code. You pay through your own bank or UPI app, using only the official payment details shown, then send the payment screenshot to the contact number shown. The admin then marks the payment as received in the app. A payment counts only once we have received it. We are not responsible for money paid any other way.
  • When you pay. Monthly fees are due by the 2nd of the month. Classes may be paused until unpaid fees are received.
  • What you pay for. Fees buy a number of classes for one subject for one named student. Prices follow our fee setup at the time of the request, including any discount for larger packs. We may revise fees and plans, and will tell you in advance.
  • Validity and expiry. Classes are valid from the payment date for: up to 30 classes, 120 days; 31 to 50, 180 days; 51 to 60, 240 days; 61 to 100, 365 days; otherwise 180 days unless the fee request says different. The app shows the expiry date on each payment, and that day itself still counts. Unused classes expire after that date and are not refunded.
  • Missed classes. Tell the admin and the teacher at least 4 hours before a class you cannot attend; it can then be rescheduled within two weeks, subject to teacher availability. A class missed without notice counts as attended and is taken from your balance. A class cancelled by us or the teacher is rescheduled at no cost to your balance.
  • Transfers. Classes cannot be passed to another student. Moving unused classes between subjects for the same student is at our discretion and is recorded in the app.
  • Refunds. All fees are non-refundable. Only in extreme cases, and solely at our discretion, we may agree a refund; any such refund is recorded in the app against the classes returned. Asking for a refund does not mean one will be given.
  • Records. The app's records of classes, payments, refunds and balances are what we use to settle any question. If you think a record is wrong, tell us within 30 days.
  • Bank or UPI charges on your side are yours to pay.

5. Teachers: classes, attendance and pay

Teachers are independent contractors paid per class taught, under the Teaching Services Agreement. These Terms cover only use of the app. If the two differ, the Teaching Services Agreement wins.

  • Teachers must log attendance in the app every teaching day, with the correct student, date, subject and mode (online or offline). A missed entry must be reported to LIB Education within 48 hours. Students, parents and LIB Education can see attendance.
  • Pay is worked out from the classes logged in the app and the agreed rate for each category, type and mode, and is paid by the 5th of the following month. Salary slips made by the app are a record of that calculation.
  • Leave and availability entered in the app help us plan classes; they do not by themselves guarantee classes or pay.
  • Teachers see student contact details and class information only to teach those students. They must not copy, share or use it for anything else, and must not solicit LIB Education students during their engagement or for 12 months after it ends.
  • Material teachers create or upload for LIB Education classes (resources, papers, briefs) belongs to LIB Education.
  • Teachers cannot see student fee or payment details; the app keeps money matters for the admin.

6. Homework, practice papers, photos and uploads

Students may submit typed answers and photos of written work for homework and practice papers. Teachers may upload resources, textbooks and pictures.

  • Upload only your own work, or material you have the right to share. Do not upload photos of other people, ID documents, or anything unrelated to study.
  • By uploading, you allow LIB Education and the relevant teachers to store, view, mark and comment on it for tuition.
  • Multiple-choice answers are marked automatically. A teacher may change these marks on review, and written answers are marked by the teacher. Marks and feedback in the app are for practice only and are not official exam results.
  • Practice papers use past board papers and other questions from our question bank. Answers in the bank may have been prepared with help from AI and checked by teachers; they may still contain mistakes. Tell your teacher if you spot one.
  • Resources a teacher shares are for the enrolled students and parents only. Do not pass them on outside My LIB.

7. AI features

Some parts of the app use artificial intelligence: the Help bot, the AI chat assistant, study plan suggestions, and the teacher Research tab (lesson prep briefs). These are provided through Anthropic's Claude service.

  • What you type into an AI feature, and any text the feature needs to answer (for example chapter names or a study plan), is sent to the AI provider to produce the answer. Do not type personal details, passwords or sensitive information into AI features.
  • AI answers can be wrong, out of date or incomplete. Check them before relying on them. They are not a substitute for a teacher's judgement.
  • We turn AI on or off per role and per person, and each person has a daily limit on AI use.
  • We log each AI use (who, when, which feature, and the size of the request) to manage cost and limits. We do not keep a copy of your question in that log.
