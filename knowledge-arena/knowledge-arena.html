❔ How do the games and subjects work?

Choose a game and Knowledge Arena will automatically select suitable questions from the subjects configured for that game and your grade. You do not need to choose a subject.

Grade: if asked, choose your current school grade. This only helps select questions and does not change your LIB student profile.

Daily challenge: a mixed set of suitable questions for your grade when enough content is available. You can try it more than once.

Game XP is separate from attendance XP and rewards. Correct answers earn 5 XP; a perfect attempt earns a 10 XP bonus, subject to the daily cap.

If a game says no published questions are available, try another game or return later. It is a content-availability message, not a subscription restriction.
